Trade and economic developments
- Canada and the UAE signed a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement expected to enter into force in 2027, eliminating or reducing tariffs on more than 99% of current Canadian exports to the UAE. The agreement targets a doubling of bilateral trade to C$7 billion over the next decade and broadens the UAE’s tariff and investment network in energy, machinery, aerospace, critical minerals and agri-food. — Source: Government of Canada, Oct 27. ([pm.gc.ca](https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/10/27/prime-minister-carney-launches-new-strategic-partnership-uae-welcomes))

Geopolitical and security

Industry and strategic moves
- DP World signed an MoU with Arctic Gateway Group and CentrePort Canada to explore an integrated corridor linking the Port of Churchill, Hudson Bay Railway and Winnipeg’s inland port with DP World’s global network. No investment or construction timetable was announced, but the agreement gives DP World a potential position in an emerging Arctic gateway for grain, potash and critical-mineral exports. — Source: Government of Canada, Oct 27.
